English

In short: GoAbacUs does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. It works fully offline, has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no third-party services.

This policy describes the GoAbacUs app for Android and iOS (the “app”), developed by Mikhail Gubko (the “developer”, “we”). GoAbacUs is a learning abacus for children aged 6 and up.

1. Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for a name, e-mail address, age, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it does not create user accounts.

2. What the app uses on your device

  • Motion sensor (accelerometer). When tilt is turned on, the device’s tilt makes the beads slide, like a real abacus. The readings are used in memory only, are not saved and never leave the device. No permission is needed for this.
  • Vibration. The app vibrates gently when beads hit the frame (Android permission VIBRATE).
  • App settings. The chosen interface language is saved in the app’s private storage on the device. It is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Numbers on the abacus, examples and exercise results exist only while the app is running and are not saved or sent anywhere.

3. Internet and third parties

The app does not request Internet access and cannot send any data. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting, social login, or other third-party SDKs, and no links to external websites or purchases.

Google Play and the Apple App Store, from which you install the app, process data under their own privacy policies. That is outside the app and outside our control.

4. Children

The app is made for children aged 6 and up. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. It is safe to use without an adult setting up an account, and nothing a child does in the app is shared.

5. Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on our side. To remove everything the app stored on your device, uninstall the app.

6. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.

7. Contact

Mikhail Gubko. Questions about this policy: github.com/mgubko/GoAbacUs/issues.
